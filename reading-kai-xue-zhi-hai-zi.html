Reading 09

开学，致孩子

唐师曾

唐师曾的孩子上学那年，他给孩子写了这篇文章。我深深认同，当年就记了下来。唐先生于2026年8月23日逝世，谨以此页缅怀他。

When Tang Shizeng’s child started school, he wrote this essay for him. I deeply agreed with it and kept a copy all those years ago. Mr. Tang passed away on August 23, 2026. This page is dedicated to his memory.

  1. 孩子，你一定要学会做饭。这与伺候人无关。当关爱你的人都不在身边的时候，能生活自理。独立生存，善待自己。

    My child, you must learn to cook. It has nothing to do with serving others. When those who care for you are not by your side, you must be able to look after yourself—to live independently and treat yourself well.

  2. 孩子，学会走路之后，要学会爬树、上房、跳墙、越野、登山、滑冰、骑车、开车……旅行的方式与身份无关。任何时候，都能拔腿去自己想去的地方，不求任何人，独立、自尊地自由行走。

    My child, once you have learnt to walk, learn to climb trees and rooftops, scale walls, cross open country, climb mountains, skate, cycle and drive. The way you travel has nothing to do with status. At any moment, be able to set off for wherever you wish without depending on anyone—to move freely, independently and with self-respect.

  3. 孩子，一定要学会游泳。游过大江、大海的人，会懂得什么才是伟大。

    My child, you must learn to swim. Those who have crossed great rivers and open seas will understand what greatness truly means.

  4. 孩子，你一定要上大学，正规的大学（University），接受宇宙（Universal普世）教育。这与学历无关，技术教育让人狭隘。人生中需要经历四年本科，无拘无束又浸染书香，不可或缺。一旦走进社会，就进入了商品市场，永无安宁之日。

    My child, you must attend a proper university and receive a universal education. This is not about obtaining a qualification. A purely technical education can narrow the mind. Four undergraduate years—free, open and steeped in books—are an indispensable part of life. Once you enter society, you enter the marketplace, and tranquillity will be hard to find again.

  5. 孩子，足迹有多远，心就有多宽。心宽，你才会快乐。万一无力远行，就让书籍带你走。拓宽视野，借助读书和常识让思想远行。

    My child, the farther your footsteps carry you, the broader your heart will become. Only with a broad heart can you be happy. If you cannot travel far, let books carry you. Widen your horizons, and let reading and common sense take your mind on distant journeys.

  6. 孩子，如果世界上仅剩两碗水，一碗用来喝，一碗要用来洗干净你的脸和内衣裤。保持自己的脸和小JJ绝对干净。自尊与贫富无关，男人要常换衬衣。

    My child, if only two bowls of water remain in the world, drink one and use the other to wash your face and underclothes. Keep your face and your private parts impeccably clean. Self-respect has nothing to do with wealth or poverty, and a man should change his shirt often.

  7. 孩子，要热爱动物，美丽的或不美丽的。

    My child, love animals, whether beautiful or not.

  8. 孩子，天塌下来都别哭，也别抱怨，平静地承受。否则，只能让爱你的人更心痛。

    My child, even if the sky falls, do not cry or complain. Bear it calmly. Otherwise, you will only cause greater pain to those who love you.

  9. 孩子，就算吃酱油拌饭，也要铺上干净的餐巾，优雅地坐着。把简陋的生活过得很讲究。风度与境遇无关。

    My child, even if your meal is no more than rice with soy sauce, lay out a clean napkin and sit with grace. Live even a modest life with care and refinement. Dignity has nothing to do with circumstance.

  10. 孩子，去远方的时候，除了相机，记得带上纸笔。风景是相同的，看风景的心情千差万别、永不重复。徐霞客之所以是徐霞客，不是因为成天走路。随时观察自然、社会，绝不仅是游山玩水。要接近智者，自嘲、自省、自趣，培养希腊、罗马的“壮游”习惯。

    My child, when you travel far, remember to take pen and paper as well as a camera. The landscape may remain the same, but the state of mind with which we see it varies endlessly and is never repeated. Xu Xiake became Xu Xiake not simply because he spent his days travelling. Observe nature and society at all times; travel is far more than sightseeing. Seek the company of wise people, learn self-mockery, self-examination and self-delight, and cultivate the Greek and Roman tradition of the grand tour.

  11. 孩子，一定要有属于自己的空间，那怕只有五平方米。可以让你在和爱人吵架赌气出走的时候，不至于流落街头遭遇歹人。更重要的是，在你浮躁的时候，有个地方让你独自静下来，给自己的心一个安放的角落。人格独立，思想自由。

    My child, you must have a space of your own, even if it is only five square metres. If you leave after an argument with the person you love, it will keep you from finding yourself stranded in the street and exposed to danger. More importantly, when you feel restless, it gives you somewhere to be alone and grow calm—a corner in which your heart can come to rest. Keep your character independent and your mind free.

  12. 孩子，小孩的时候要有见识，长大的时候要有经历，你才会有个精致的人生！读别人的经验，长自己的经历。

    My child, gain perspective while you are young and experience as you grow older; only then can you build a life of depth and refinement. Read about the experience of others, and develop experience of your own.

  13. 孩子，广谱教育、教学大纲往往得照顾全局。有机会多接近各门大师，兼听则明，真言不传六耳。当然，实践第一。

    My child, broad education and formal curricula must often serve the needs of the whole. Whenever possible, learn directly from masters in many fields. Listen to different voices and your understanding will become clearer; the deepest truths are often shared only in close confidence. Above all, put knowledge into practice.

  14. 孩子，无论什么时候，都要做一个善良的人。帮助那些需要帮助的人，这样上天才能恩宠你。拥有善良，会让你成为最受上天眷顾的人。这种眷顾未必是财富与权势。善有善报，所报者，爱也。

    My child, whatever the circumstances, be a kind person. Help those who need help, and heaven may look kindly upon you. Kindness will make you one of the truly blessed, though that blessing may not take the form of wealth or power. Goodness is rewarded, and its reward is love.

  15. 孩子，儒以文乱法，而侠则以武犯禁。男人要有《游侠列传》的侠肝义胆，“竹林七贤”的士大夫情操。路见不平，拔刀相助。在齐太史简，在晋董狐笔。搁古代叫“仗义”，关公流芳百世，不是因为有钱、官大，而是挂印封金。永不背叛朋友，特别是失势的朋友。不求锦上添花，必须雪中送炭。

    My child, the scholar may challenge the law with words, while the knight-errant may cross its bounds through action. A man should possess the courage and loyalty of the wandering heroes, and the integrity of the Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove. When you encounter injustice, step forward to help. Honour those who recorded truth without fear. In earlier times this was called righteousness. Guan Yu is remembered not for wealth or rank, but for refusing riches and remaining loyal. Never betray a friend, especially one who has fallen from favour. Do not merely add flowers to brocade; bring charcoal in the snow.

  16. 孩子，相信常识，质疑一切。怀疑是科学的开始。

    My child, trust common sense and question everything. Doubt is the beginning of science.

  17. 孩子，要严守国家法律，宪法、联合国宪章。遵从人类已有的法律道德。

    My child, observe the laws of your country, its constitution and the Charter of the United Nations. Respect the legal and moral principles humanity has established.

  18. 孩子，必须有宗教情怀。有严密逻辑推理且能用实验重复的，是科学；有严密逻辑推理但不能用实验重复的，是哲学；既无严密逻辑推理又无法用实验重复的，是神学。

    My child, you must have a sense of the religious. What follows rigorous logic and can be reproduced through experiment is science; what follows rigorous logic but cannot be reproduced experimentally is philosophy; what can be neither rigorously reasoned nor experimentally reproduced is theology.

  19. 孩子，一定要珍惜时间，敬畏生命。爱惜自己的身体，也尊重别人的身体，注意卫生。人类所有宗教都从戒杀开始，反对杀戮，反对伤人，也反对自伤。

    My child, cherish time and hold life in reverence. Care for your own body, respect the bodies of others and pay attention to hygiene. Every human religion begins by forbidding the taking of life: oppose killing, oppose harming others and oppose harming yourself.

  20. 孩子，笑容、优雅、自信，是最大的财富。拥有了他们，你就会拥有一切。

    My child, a smile, grace and confidence are the greatest forms of wealth. Possess them, and you will possess everything.
