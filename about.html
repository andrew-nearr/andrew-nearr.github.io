Andrew Huiqing Ji纪会卿

中文

我是 Andrew Huiqing Ji（纪会卿），一名现在生活和工作在纽约的同城配送创业者和房地产开发者。

我出生于中国烟台，在北京求学并参加工作，先后从事过媒体科技、移动出行和房地产等领域的工作，并于 2022 年来到美国。

今天，我在纽约经营同城配送和建筑业务。我关注城市、房地产、科技、商业和家庭，关注普通人如何通过经年累月、细小而有意识的努力，建立有意义的人生。

我记录自己建造的东西、读过的书、学到的知识，也记录那些塑造了我的人和经历。

如果一定要用一句话来定义自己：

我建造——建筑、事业，以及自己的人生。

English

I’m Andrew Huiqing Ji, a local delivery entrepreneur and real estate developer living and working in New York.

I was born in Yantai, China, and studied and began my career in Beijing. Over the years, I have worked across media and technology, mobility, and real estate. In 2022, I moved to the United States.

Today, I run a local delivery business and develop buildings in New York. I am interested in cities, real estate, technology, business, and family—and in how ordinary people can build meaningful lives through years of small, deliberate efforts.

I document the things I build, the books I read, and the lessons I learn. I also write about the people and experiences that have shaped me.

If I had to define myself in one sentence:

I build—buildings, businesses, and my own life.
