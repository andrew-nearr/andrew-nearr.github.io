Project 02 · Completed

Hampton Condo

42-09 Hampton Street
Elmhurst, NY 11373

Hampton Condo is the first building I completed in New York—and the project through which I truly learned how the city’s real estate industry works.

Working through its development gave me a systematic understanding of the relationships among developers, architects, engineers, general contractors, subcontractors, suppliers, the Department of Buildings, Con Edison, brokerages, and property management. In my mind, these participants and processes came together as a complete map of real estate development and operations.

The knowledge and practical know-how I gained here became a turning point, helping me move from a food-delivery business toward real estate development and operations.

Project 03 · Completed

GBJ 12 Realty

150-18 12th Road
Whitestone, NY 11357

GBJ 12 Realty is a mixed-use development in Whitestone, bringing ground-floor commercial spaces together with residences above.

I participated in every stage of this project—from land acquisition, planning, zoning, and design through construction, interior finishing, sales, and property management.

Following the project across its full life cycle gave me a complete, practical understanding of real estate development: not as a series of separate tasks, but as one continuous process from an idea and a piece of land to a finished building and its long-term operation.
