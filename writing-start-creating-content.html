写给 Roy 和 Tina：现在开始创作内容

To Roy and Tina: Start Creating Content Now

Andrew Huiqing JiAugust 2026中文 / English

Hi，Roy and Tina：

当 Roy 跟随郑渊洁学习中文写作时，郑渊洁曾在与我聊天时提醒我：“要多写文章。”写文章，才有影响力。

后来，我写了关于“电动车进北京”的系列文章。郑渊洁评价说：“写得很勤奋，但是不吸引人。”有一次，他和周航一起吃饭时还提起这件事，也和我分享了一些写作技巧。

现在，“多写”的含义可能已经不只是写文章，还应该包括播客、视频以及其他形式的内容创作。

最近我越来越体会到：你们现在就应该有意识地多做内容，逐渐积攒自己的注意力。不要因为害怕受到关注后可能招来攻击，就停下创作的脚步。

一、创作可以整理思维

写作本身就是思考的一部分。

作家丁雷在给自己孩子的信中写过：“写作不仅锻炼你的文笔，同时整理你的思想。”

当你试着把一个念头写清楚、讲清楚时，往往也会发现自己原来没有想明白的地方。创作不只是表达思考，也在帮助思考发生。

二、内容是有复利的

好的内容不会轻易消失。它会一直留在那里，保存着你当时的心情、处境和理解。而这些东西一旦错过那个时间点，就再也无法原样重现。

一篇文章、一段播客或一段视频，可能在创作完成时并没有产生明显影响，却可能在未来某个无法预知的时刻，为你带来有价值的连接、帮助或机会。

三、持续创作会积攒注意力

资源和资金会随着注意力流动。

如果平时没有积攒注意力，等到需要资源、需要资金时，就只能一次又一次地向投资人、客户证明自己。相反，如果长期认真创作，让别人逐渐了解你、信任你，资源和资金有时会主动找到你。

这一点，我在寻找租客时也有所体会。我曾和 Roy 分享过：“好租客是等来的。”很多重要的机会也是如此——先把自己该做的事做好，持续发出信号，然后耐心等待合适的人出现。

我计划开始更认真地创作内容。

我也期待看到你们的作品。

Dad

English Translation

To Roy and Tina: Start Creating Content Now

Hi, Roy and Tina,

When Roy was learning Chinese writing from Zheng Yuanjie, he once reminded me during a conversation: “You should write more.” Writing, he said, is how you build influence.

Later, when I wrote a series of articles about bringing electric vehicles into Beijing, Zheng Yuanjie told me, “You write diligently, but the articles are not engaging.” He even brought this up once over a meal with Zhou Hang and shared some writing techniques with me.

Today, “writing more” probably means more than articles. It should also include podcasts, videos, and other forms of content creation.

Lately, I have come to believe more strongly that you should begin creating deliberately now and gradually build an audience of your own. Do not let the fear that attention may also bring criticism stop you from creating.

1. Creating Organizes Your Thinking

Writing is itself part of thinking.

In a letter to his child, the writer Ding Lei once wrote: “Writing not only strengthens your prose; it also organizes your thoughts.”

When you try to express an idea clearly, you often discover the parts you have not yet fully understood. Creation does not merely communicate thought; it helps thought take shape.

2. Content Compounds

Good content does not simply disappear. It remains, preserving your state of mind, your circumstances, and your understanding at that particular moment. Once that moment passes, those elements can never be recreated in exactly the same way.

An article, podcast, or video may have little visible impact when it is first published, yet at some unpredictable point in the future, it may bring you a valuable connection, assistance, or opportunity.

3. Consistent Creation Builds Attention

Resources and capital follow attention.

If you have not built attention over time, then when you need resources or funding, you must repeatedly prove yourself to investors and customers. But if you create thoughtfully for years—allowing people to know and trust you—resources and capital may begin to find their way to you.

I have felt something similar when looking for tenants. I once told Roy, “A good tenant is someone you wait for.” Many important opportunities work the same way: first do your work well, keep sending out signals, and then wait patiently for the right people to appear.

I plan to begin creating more seriously.

I look forward to seeing what both of you create, too.

Dad
