Reading 01

The Power Broker

Robert Moses and the Fall of New York

I believe this is a book every new immigrant to New York should read.

It allows me to experience more than a century of New York’s growth and transformation from a broad historical perspective, and to understand how this city truly works. Whatever work one does, that understanding helps one move beyond simply living here—to feel the city, understand it, belong to it, help build it, and make it better.

Author
Robert A. Caro
Published
1974
Subject
New York · Power · Urban Development
