Reading 01

The Three-Body Problem

Remembrance of Earth’s Past Trilogy · 三体三部曲

Few works of science fiction have drawn me in as deeply as Cixin Liu’s three-volume series. I have recommended it to my children, too.

The trilogy has found countless readers in both China and the United States. Former U.S. President Barack Obama was among them; he praised its imagination and immense scope. Its vision of civilization, time, survival, and the universe continues to stay with me.

Among the well-known Chinese writers whose work I know, Cixin Liu and Yuanjie Zheng stand apart in one particular way: in their writing, I do not see the familiar opening of “I was very poor as a child.” Their imaginations are not confined by poverty. Perhaps that is one reason their work feels so expansive to me.

I hope my children will be prosperous in the future—not only materially, but also in imagination, confidence, and possibility. I know Yuanjie Zheng. I hope that one day life will also give me the chance to meet Cixin Liu and thank him in person.
